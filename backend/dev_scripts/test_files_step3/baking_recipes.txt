Artisanal Sourdough Bread Baking Guide

To bake a perfect sourdough bread, combine organic bread flour, water, salt, and active wild yeast starter. Bulk fermentation should occur at 24 degrees Celsius for 4 to 6 hours. Bake inside a preheated Dutch oven at 230°C for 20 minutes with lid on, then 20 minutes uncovered for a crispy golden crust.
